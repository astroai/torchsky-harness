## Published UPDC metric meaning

The r2 map report compares iter10 with iter0 on common support after both products pass through `torchsky.map.coadd_image_plane`. Its metric categories are:

- Weighted image agreement: RMSE/MAE, residual bias and standard deviation, target/prediction RMS, plus residual RMSE after block scales 1, 2, and 4.
- Support and error: support fraction/weight, coverage and zero-support fraction, mean weight, reconstruction RMSE/MAE, p95 and maximum absolute error, and per-band reconstruction RMSE.
- Aggregate flux: total and per-band flux values/errors/ratios, color-band ratios, and a common-support pixel-sum ratio against iter0.
- Static sharpness: finite-difference gradient energy for prediction and reference, and their ratio.

The original `total_flux_relative_error` uses a fixed absolute denominator floor that biases the low-flux estimate downward. Use the separately completed unit-aware Jy integrity result for that diagnostic; the original published-metrics JSON remains unchanged.

Names containing `residual_snr` or `signal_to_residual` are ratios of image RMS to image residual RMSE (and dB transforms). They measure agreement with iter0, **not source-detection SNR**. Gradient energy and Laplacian/sharpness ratios are proxies, **not effective PSFs or calibrated resolution**. Neither product is a known-truth reference. Bilinear resampling may change small-scale noise and apparent structure. The archived `updec-pair.json` uses an analytic linear-TAN/bilinear 768-pixel crop and reports pairwise RMSE/MAE, crop/common flux ratios, gradient/Laplacian ratios, WCS/header metadata, and read time; its WCS grids differ.

The r2 report sets `depth_claim=false` and `reference_is_truth=false`. Its artifact root is `/arc/projects/ots/torchsky-runs/smacs-inr-autonomous-20261010/smacs-published-updc-map-metrics-20261011-r2/`. The published products remain on CANFAR as the final artifacts; this checkout has no executable product copy. The prior r1 attempt failed at import before reading inputs.

The separate `smacs-published-updc-best-stack-20261011-r1` report contains 12 rows (iter0 and iter10 for each band) against archived SWarp Lanczos3 on common support. It adds support, weighted residual/image agreement, aggregate and per-band/color flux, negative-pixel/negative-to-positive flux, and static sharpness diagnostics. Its comparison reference is an archived stacker result, not known truth; its `residual_snr` fields are image-agreement ratios and do not measure source-detection SNR.
